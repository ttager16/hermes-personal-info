About Privacy

Personal desktop assistant

Help with everyday tasks, on your own computer.

Hermes Personal is an independent, privately operated integration using the open-source Hermes Agent. It is intended for its operator and explicitly authorized, personally known users. It is not a public sign-up service.

Why it connects to Google

The Gmail integration helps a connected user find and read messages, prepare drafts and replies, send requested messages, and organize mail with labels, archiving and trash. Available actions depend on the permissions that user has granted and the configured tools.

Gmail mail-management access is requested for those features. Basic Google account identity identifies the connected account. The integration does not obtain access to Calendar, Drive, Contacts or other Google services through a Gmail grant; additional services require separate configuration and consent.

A public information page for a private integration

The assistant and its local integrations run on the operator's computer. This site provides information about the application and its data practices; it does not host a mailbox interface, an assistant session or account credentials.

Read the privacy policy for details about access, use, storage, sharing and revocation of Google data.

Operator and contact

This integration is operated by the owner of the Hermes Personal information repository. For support or privacy questions, contact the operator through that repository. Issues are public: do not include message contents, credentials or other private information.

Hermes Personal is not an official Google or Nous Research product and is not endorsed by either organization.
